# Vocabulary

**Biotic (factor)**: Living parts in an ecosystem
**Abiotic (factor)**: Non-living parts in an ecosystem

**Producer**: An organism that creates its own energy using energy from the environment (energy from **Abiotic factors**)
**Consumer**: An organism that has to eat other organisms for energy (can not produce their own energy)
	- **Herbivore**: A consumer that exclusively consumes plant matter
	- **Carnivore**: A consumer that exclusively consumes animal matter
	- **Omnivore**: A consumer that consumes both animal and plant matter
**Decomposers**: An organism that breaks down already dead organic material to create energy

**Autotroph**: Another name for a producer (produces energy from nonliving things)
**Photoautotroph**: Autotrophs/Producers that use sunlight to produce and bring energy into an ecosystem through **Photosynthesis**
