## What is Included in Driver's Ed?
- 30 hours of in-class(room) instruction
- 6 hours behind the wheel with an instructor
- 6 hours of in-car observation
	- Watching another student perform his/her lesson
	- Can negate if 8 or more behind the wheel hours are completed
---
## Getting the Instruction (Learner's) Permit 
##### Requirements
- Must be 15
- Must be officially enrolled in a government approved driver's ed course
- See [At the DMV](#at-the-dmv)
##### At the DMV
- Must pass a vision screening
- Must pass a written test based on the *Rules of the Road*
- What to bring:
	- Proof of enrollment in your driver's ed course
		- Given by the driving school
	- Identification documents, such as
		- Any document containing your official signature, such as:
			- Debit/Credit cards
			- Canceled checks
			- State-issued IDs
			- Legal/Financial docs
			- Any other official cards
		- Birth certificate
		- Passport
		- State issued ID
			- Covers the written signature, DOB and 1 of the 2 Illinois residency forms
	- Social Security Card
	- Two distinct (different) proofs of residency
		- School transcript
		- Report cards
	- A parent/legal guardian to sign consent forms
	- Simple list:
		- IL state ID
			- Covers Groups A (Signature), B (Birthdate), and half of D (Residency)
		- Physical SS card
			- Covers Group C (Social Security)
		- School transcript OR report card
			- Covers the other half of Group D (Residency)

## With a Permit
##### Requirements while You Have a Permit
- Drive with a qualified adult (at least 21 years old), sitting in the front passenger seat
- Log all practice hours
	- Must complete a minimum of 50 practice hours, 10 of which must be done at night
- Hold permit for 9 months
- Maintain a perfect record for the later 6 months of the 9 month period
- Teen Driving Curfew:
	- Cannot drive 10 PM to 6 AM on Sunday through Thursday
	- Cannot drive 11 PM to 6 AM on Friday and Saturday
