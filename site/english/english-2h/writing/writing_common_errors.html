Common Error in Writing

  • All errors can be fixed through thorough proofreading

  • Keep tense constant (eg: past, present, future)

  • Subject-Verb agreement

    • Remove all prepositional phrases in the sentence
    • Match the number of the subject and verb (singular or plural)

  • Commas

    • Commas separate:

      • Elements within sentence
      • An introductory (subordinating) clause or phrase
      • Phrases and clauses within a sentence
    • Commas also set of an appositive
    • Commas enclose lesser information that interrupts a sentence
    • Commas separate words in a series (eg: I love to bake, run, and cook)

  • Run-ons and Fragments

    • Run-ons:

      • Break apart into multiple sentences
      • Use a coordinating conjunction to break the sentence
      • Use a subordinating conjunction to break the sentence
      • Use a semicolon to break the sentence
    • For fragments, you must add either a subject or predicate to complete the sentence.
