
### Equation of Cellular Respiration

$C_{2}H_{12}O_{6} + 6CO_{2}$ ---> $6CO_{2} + 6H_{2}O + ATP$

**Conservation of Matter**
- No atoms are destroyed or created in the reaction
### Glycolysis

- **Reactants**: Glucose; ATP; ADP
- **Products**: NADH; ATP; Pyruvate
- **Location**: Cytoplasm
- **Purpose**: Break glucose into two pyruvate
- Anaerobic
- 10 Steps
	1. Glucose is merged with 2 phosphates from 2 ATP, creating Fructose 1,6-bisphosphate
	2. The fructose is broken into DHAP and glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate, small (3 carbon) sugars
		- The DHAP will be converted into glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate
	(Everything beyond this point happens twice)
	3. NAD$^{+}$ is deoxidized, gaining an electron from the glyceraldehyde
	4. 2 ADP are converted to ATP using the phosphates from the 2 small sugars

### Link Reaction

- **Reactants**: Pyruvate; COA-SH; NAD+
- **Products**: $CO_{2}$; NADH; AcetylCOA
- **Location**: Matrix of the mitochondria
- Aerobic
- One step
