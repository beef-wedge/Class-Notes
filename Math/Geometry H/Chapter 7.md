## Section 7.1
##### Triangle Sum Theorem
- The sum of the measure of angles in a triangle is 180 degrees

##### Exterior Angle theorem
- The measure of an exterior angle of a triangle is equal to the sum of the remote interior angles

##### Midline Theorem
- A segment joining the midpoints of 2 sides of a triangle
- This segment is parallel to the third side and its length is one half of the third side
---
## Section 7.2
##### No Choice Theorem
- If 2 angles of one triangle are congruent  to 2 corresponding angles of a second triangle, the third angles are congruent
- 2 triangles do not need to be congruent for no choice theorem
---
## Section 7.3
##### Sum of the Measures of the Angles of a Polygon
- Given by the formula: $A=(n-2)(180)$
- Where ***A*** is the total of all angles in the polygon and ***n*** is the number of sides

##### Sum of the Measures of the Exterior Angles
- Always 360 degrees

##### Diagonals of a polygon
- Given by the formula: $D=\frac{n(n-3)}{2}$
- Where ***n*** is the number of sides and ***D*** is the number of diagonals
---
## Section 7.4
##### Regular Polygons
- Polygons which are both equilateral and equilangular

##### Exterior Angles of Regular Polygons
- Given by the formula: $E=\frac{360}{n}$
- Where ***E*** is the measure of each exterior angle and ***n*** is the number of sides
