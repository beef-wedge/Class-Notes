## Section 3.1 - What are Congruent Figures?
##### Congruent Figures
- Two geometric figures are **congruent** if one of them could be mapped (places) onto the other through rigid motions (translations, rotations)
- Congruent figures have the same shape and size
- All angles and side measures must be the same
##### Congruent Triangles
- Congruent triangles are formed only if all sum parts are congruent
##### Congruency Statements
- In congruency statements, order matters
- Corresponding letters must be in the same order
##### Reflexive Property
- Everything is congruent to itself
---
## Section 3.2 - Three Ways to Prove Triangles Congruent
##### SSS (Side Side Side)
- If all three sides of a triangle are congruent, the whole triangle is congruent
- Formally: IF there exists a correspondence between the vertices of 2 triangles such that 3 sides of one triangle are congruent to the other triangle, the two triangles are congruent
##### SAS (Side Angle Side)
- If 2 sides and an angle are congruent to that of another triangle's, then the triangles are congruent
- The angle must lie adjacent to both sides
- Formally: If there exists a correspondence between the vertices of 2 triangles such that 2 sides and the included angle of one triangle are congruent to the corresponding parts of the other triangle, the two triangles are congruent
##### ASA (Angle Side Angle)
- If 2 angles and a side are congruent to that of another triangle's, then the triangles are congruent
- The side must lie adjacent to both angles
- Formally: If there exists a correspondence between the vertices of 2 triangles such that 2 angles and the included side of one triangle are congruent to the corresponding parts of the other triangle, the 2 triangles are congruent

## Section 3.3 - CPCTC and Circles
##### CPCTC
- Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles are Congruent
##### Circles Review
- Circumference is the perimeter of a circle
- Radius is the distance from the center of a circle to any point on the circumference
- All radii (plural of radius) of a circle and congruent

## Section 3.4 - Beyond CPCTC
##### Medians of Triangles
- A line segment drawn from any vertex of a triangle to the midpoint of the opposite side
##### Altitudes of Triangles
- A Segment drawn from any vertex of a triangle to the opposite side and perpendicular to that side

## Section 3.5 - Overlapping Triangles
##### Steps for Proofs With Overlapping Triangles
1. Look for different triangle pairs of congruent
2. Identify which can be proven
3. Prove triangles congruent
4. Use CPCTC
5. Go beyond, if necessary

## Section 3.6 - Types of Triangles
##### Classify by Sides
- Scalene:
	- No congruent sides
- Isosceles: 
	- 2 congruent sides
	- Legs are the congruent sides
	- Base is the non-congruent side
- Equilateral:
	- All sides congruent
##### Classify by Angles
- Equliangular:
	- All angles congruent
- Acute:
	- All angles are acute
- Right:
	- Contains one right angles
	- Other angles are acute
- Obtuse:
	- One angle obtuse, others are acute
- All triangles have at least 2 acute angles
---
## Section 3.7 - Angle Side Theorem
##### Side-Angle Theorem
- If 2 sides are congruent, then their opposite angles are as well
##### Angle-Side Theorem
- If 2 angles are congruent, then their opposite sides are as well
##### Two Ways to Prove a Triangle is Isosceles
1. If at least 2 sides are congruent, then the triangle is isosceles
2. If at least 2 angles are congruent, then the triangle is isosceles
##### Triangle Inequality Theorem Extension
- Formally: If two sides of a triangle are not congruent, then the angles opposite them are not congruent, and the larger angle is opposite the longer side
- Formally: If the angles of a triangle are not congruent, then the sides opposite them are not congruent, and the longer side is opposite the large angle.
---
## Section 3.8 - The HL Postulate
##### The Hypotenuse Leg Postulate (HL)
- Formally: If there exists a correspondence between the vertices of 2 tight triangles such that the hypotenuse and a leg of one triangle are congruent to the corresponding parts of the other triangle, then the 2 right triangles are congruent
